## Creative Direction
- Tone preset: `cinematic`
- Creative direction: Dramatic, premium fintech launch film with cultural resonance
- Interpretation: Deep moody shadows (`#07120C`), emerald horizon glow (`#087B62`), harvest gold accents (`#E6B957`), crisp typography, restrained cinematic pacing.
- Angle: From the exhausting friction of paper khatas to seamless spoken financial clarity.
- Hook: The traditional paper ledger dissolving into a luminous emerald digital interface.
- Outro: The Baki Bondhu shield crest with the DevCenterPoint hallmark.
- Avoid:
  - Generic SaaS tropes ("streamline your workflow", "all-in-one platform")
  - Abstract 3D floating blobs
  - Fake financial numbers without BDT/Paisa context
