# Brag Plan: Baki Bondhu (বাকী বন্ধু)

## What is this app?
An offline-first, voice-powered digital credit and debit ledger (Khata) engineered for Bangladeshi shopkeepers, converting natural spoken Bangla into exact financial ledger entries.

## The angle
In a world of complex, tap-heavy business software, Baki Bondhu honors the rhythm of Bangladeshi commerce: merchants talk, transactions record, and math is settled to the exact Paisa with zero internet required.

## Hook (first 2-3 seconds)
A cinematic macro view of an aging, ink-stained paper Khata dissolving into a sleek, glowing emerald mobile ledger surface: "Every evening across Bangladesh, millions of merchants face the same fragile paper."

## Key moments (the middle)
- **Natural Voice Recognition in Action**: A merchant's voice waveform pulses, live transcribing *"রহিম ভাই বাকি ৫০০ টাকা"* into an animated transaction badge with zero typing.
- **Instant Dual Ledger Calculation**: The customer's due balance counts up in real-time in Bengali script (৳ ৫৫০ থেকে ৳ ১,০৫০) with minor-unit Paisa precision.
- **Offline Resilience & Cloud Sync**: A subtle visual badge demonstrates 100% offline operation transitioning effortlessly to a verified Google-backed cloud sync indicator.

## Outro / punchline
The Baki Bondhu shield crest settles over a deep forest emerald backdrop with the signature promise:
**"আপনার দোকানের হিসাব, এখন শুধু মুখে বললেই।"** (Your business ledger, simply by speaking.)

## User flow worth showing
1. **Entry**: Merchant taps the persistent emerald microphone floating action button.
2. **Key Action**: Merchant speaks *"রহিম ভাই বাকি ৫০০ টাকা"*; the audio waveform reacts and extracts Customer: Rahim, Type: Baki (Debit), Amount: ৳500.
3. **Result**: Transaction commits with a gentle haptic snap; Rahim's ledger and the shop's daily balance update instantly.
