Paper khatas tear. Calculations fail. Baki Bondhu (বাকী বন্ধু) transforms everyday Bangladeshi shopkeeping into an effortless, offline-first voice ledger. Speak your transaction in natural Bangla, and let the ledger settle to the exact paisa.

"আপনার দোকানের হিসাব, এখন শুধু মুখে বললেই।"

Engineered with Kotlin, Jetpack Compose, Room SQLite, Credential Manager & Firebase Firestore by DevCenterPoint.

Check out the project: https://github.com/beingmushfiq/Baki-Bondhu
